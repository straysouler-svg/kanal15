Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy covers the application kanal15-publisher, a personal command-line tool operated by the author of the Kanal 15 project ("we", "the tool").

What the application does

The application uploads gameplay videos created by the author from the author's own computer to the author's own TikTok account, using the official TikTok Content Posting API. TikTok Login Kit is used solely to authorize the author's own account via OAuth 2.0.

Data we collect

None. The application does not collect, transmit, store or share any personal data of any user. It runs locally on the author's machine and communicates only with TikTok's official API endpoints.

Tokens and credentials

OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok are stored locally on the author's computer and are never shared with any third party. They are used exclusively to authenticate requests to the TikTok API.

Third parties

The application interacts only with TikTok (open.tiktokapis.com). No analytics, advertising, tracking, cookies or third-party SDKs are used. No data is sold or transferred to anyone.

Data deletion

Since no user data is collected, there is nothing to delete. TikTok authorization can be revoked at any time in TikTok account settings.
